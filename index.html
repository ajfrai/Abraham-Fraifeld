What is happening?

Bystander is a first-aid prompt for someone with no training and no time. It is based on published first-aid guidance from the American Heart Association, the European Resuscitation Council and the Red Cross. It was drafted with AI and has not been reviewed by a clinician, so a first-aid course is still the best preparation. It does not replace emergency services.

Each guide ends with an optional question: what happened? If you choose to report, it opens a public GitHub issue under your own GitHub account with the outcome, the guide you used, how long you did CPR and your country. Nothing is sent unless you submit it.
